Who I am ♡

Learning by Building 💻

Hi! I'm Samriddhi Mishra, a first-year B.Tech Computer Science Engineering student at Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Nagpur.

I'm not an industry veteran or a corporate expert—I am a student who genuinely loves the craft of software and the challenge of intelligent systems. My curiosity started with wanting to understand what happens beneath the surface of everyday digital tools, and it quickly grew into building my own autonomous assistants, analyzing neurological datasets, and experimenting with edge telemetry.

When I hit a roadblock or a bug, I don't give up. I read documentation, test small test-cases in Python or C, write research notes, and iterate until the idea comes to life.
